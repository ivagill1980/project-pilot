# Working with more than one person

Load this reference when more than one person works on the project, or when the work will
be handed to someone who was not in the session.

Assistants make solo work loops easy, so a project can quietly become one person plus a
machine. Keep the artifacts shared instead:

- **Write control state for a reader who was not there.** `STATE.md`, `MAP.md`, and
  `DECISIONS.md` use full words and avoid session shorthand, so a teammate can pick up the
  coordinate without reading the transcript.
- **Record who, once there is more than one.** Add the author or reviewer to decisions,
  evidence records, and review handoffs, so ownership is visible rather than assumed.
- **Bring deliveries back to people.** A review handoff goes to a human reviewer, not only
  to the next automated pass. Unreviewed work accumulating on a branch is the failure this
  prevents.
- **Keep decisions readable.** A teammate needs the trade-off and the condition that would
  reopen it, not only the outcome.
- **Let scoped differences stand, and resolve the rest.** Two documents with different
  scopes may legitimately disagree; two claims about the same scope may not.

## Handoff

When pausing work that someone else will continue:

- the coordinate (`Phase → Milestone → Task → Step`),
- what actually became true, with its evidence,
- what is still `? UNKNOWN` and who can resolve it,
- the next bounded action,

recorded in durable state rather than in the conversation.
